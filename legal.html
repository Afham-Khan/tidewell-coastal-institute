Privacy and terms

This site is a university assignment about a fictional institute. The notices below describe how it behaves.

Privacy notice

This website is static. It sets no cookies, runs no scripts, loads no third-party resources and has no analytics. We do not collect any personal data through it.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email application with a pre-filled message. Nothing is transmitted until you press send in that application, and the message is then handled by your email provider, not by this site.

Hosting

The site is served by a static hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security. We do not have access to these logs.

Terms of use

Fictional content

Tidewell, its people, publications, datasets, figures and addresses are invented for coursework. Do not rely on anything here for real decisions about coastal safety, sea level or water quality.

Reuse

The text and code were written for an individual assignment. If you wish to reuse any part, ask the author first.

No warranty

The site is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind.
