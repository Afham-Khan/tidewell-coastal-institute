Careers

Four roles are open this autumn. We are a small team, so each role has real scope and a named colleague to work with.

How we hire

Applications are read without names or photographs in the first round. We ask for a short note on one piece of work you are proud of; there are no cover letters. Interviews include a practical task of about an hour, and we pay for travel.

Open roles

Field technician

Port Alder · Full-time · On site

Deploy, service and recover buoys and estuary moorings from our 9-metre survey vessel, working in a team of three.

Responsibilities

  • Carry out scheduled servicing at nine stations and respond to faults within 48 hours
  • Maintain the calibration log and spares inventory
  • Support safety briefings and vessel checks

You have

  • Coxswain or equivalent boat-handling certificate
  • Comfortable with basic electronics and fault-finding
  • Willing to work some early starts and weekend tides

Research software engineer

Port Alder or remote · Full-time

Maintain the data pipeline from raw sensor files to published datasets, with a focus on reproducibility.

Responsibilities

  • Own the quality-flagging code and its tests
  • Automate dataset releases and changelogs
  • Help researchers turn analysis scripts into reusable tools

You have

  • Strong Python and data-handling experience
  • Familiarity with NetCDF or similar array formats
  • Care about documentation as much as code

Postdoctoral researcher, sea level

Port Alder · 24 months

Investigate why coastal altimetry and tide gauges diverge, building on our Harrow Sound analysis.

Responsibilities

  • Combine GNSS, gauge and altimetry records
  • Publish methods and uncertainty estimates
  • Supervise one MSc student per year

You have

  • PhD in geodesy, oceanography or a related field
  • Experience with time-series analysis
  • A record of clear writing

Communications officer

Port Alder · Part-time, 3 days

Explain our work to coastal communities, councils and schools, and edit the journal.

Responsibilities

  • Commission and edit journal articles
  • Run two school visits a term
  • Write plain-language summaries for every paper

You have

  • Experience explaining technical topics accurately
  • Confidence editing scientific text
  • Interest in coastal communities
