Frequently asked questions

Short answers about our data, methods and work. If yours is missing, write to us.

Is the data free to use?

Yes. All datasets are released under CC BY 4.0. Please cite the dataset ID and release year.

How often are datasets updated?

It varies by dataset, from daily for the estuary sensors to annual for bacteriology samples. The catalogue lists each schedule.

What is the difference between relative and absolute sea level?

Relative sea level is the water height measured against the land, as at a tide gauge. Absolute sea level is measured against the centre of the Earth, as by satellite. Our journal article on Harrow Sound explains the difference in detail.

How accurate are the low-cost sensors?

Conductivity sensors are accurate to about 0.3 practical salinity units after calibration. Published drift data lets you apply your own correction.

Do you give planning advice?

No. We describe what has been measured and how certain we are. Planning decisions belong to the authorities responsible.

Can students visit or do placements?

We host two MSc placements a year and school groups each term. Write to us through the contact page.

How do I report an error in a dataset?

Use the contact form and name the dataset ID and date range. Confirmed corrections are credited in the changelog.

Is Tidewell a real organisation?

No. Tidewell is a fictional institute created for a university assignment. All content on this site is invented.
