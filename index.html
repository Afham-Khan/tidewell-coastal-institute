Measured coasts. Open data.

Tidewell operates a network of nine monitoring buoys and tide gauges along the Alder Coast, and publishes every measurement, method and correction for anyone to check.

Research programmes

Three questions, measured carefully

Sea level and land motion

Separating the water rising from the land sinking, using tide gauges tied to a GNSS reference network.

Estuary water quality

Salinity, turbidity and bacterial load measured every ten minutes, so storm effects are visible hour by hour.

Instruments and methods

Low-cost buoy hardware, calibration procedures and drift corrections, published so other groups can reuse them.
