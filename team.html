Team

Twenty-four people work at Tidewell. These eight lead our programmes and operations.

  • IO

    Imani Okafor

    Director, estuary programme

    Physical oceanographer. Leads the Harrow Sound network and chairs the data release board.

  • PL

    Per Lindqvist

    Head of sea-level research

    Geodesist working on tide gauges and GNSS. Maintains the Alder Point record.

  • MR

    Maria Reyes

    Data engineer

    Builds the quality-flagging pipeline and the public dataset releases.

  • SB

    Samuel Brandt

    Instrument lead

    Designs the buoy hardware and runs the calibration laboratory.

  • NH

    Nadia Haddad

    Senior technician

    Leads field deployments and station maintenance from the survey vessel.

  • KT

    Kenji Tanaka

    Research scientist

    Studies bathing-water compliance and links rainfall to bacterial load.

  • EP

    Eleni Papadakis

    Communications and outreach

    Writes the journal and plain-language summaries; runs school visits.

  • TF

    Tomas Ferreira

    Operations manager

    Looks after finance, safety and the relationship with harbour authorities.
